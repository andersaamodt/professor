# The reviewed catalog and the open frontier

`categories.yaml` names recurring pedagogical questions. `tacks.yaml` contains
reversible maneuvers, not recipes or learner types. An AI may extend either
catalog autonomously only by adding a complete **provisional** record; the
constitutional floor always wins.

`reviewed.sha256` pins the canonical projection of every `active` category and
`active` tack. Provisional additions do not alter the digest. Promoting,
retiring, or materially revising an active record does, so lint fails until a
human-reviewed release deliberately updates the pin. The digest is a tripwire
and review receipt, not an oracle of pedagogical quality.

Evidence tiers describe only the bounded mechanism and outcome stated in a
tack. A lineage is not an experiment; one strong component does not certify a
compound design; one learner cannot establish a general effect. Read each
source's declared use and limits.
