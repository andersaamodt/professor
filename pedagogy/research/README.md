# Professor's scholarly metabolism

This directory is where Professor keeps learning how to teach. It is public,
general, and Git-safe: no learner histories, session language, private
experiments, campaign state, credentials, downloaded papers, book scans, or
transcripts belong here.

## The research loop

`agenda.yaml` holds consequential questions rather than favorite answers. A
host runs `bin/professor research`, searches current and foundational work, and
adds a structured note under `notes/`. Notes distinguish reading depth,
methods, populations, findings, interpretations, limits, contradiction, and
non-implications. The loop is:

> notice a gap → bound a question → search plural routes → read honestly →
> compare claims → write a working synthesis → seek disconfirmation → propose
> separately → revisit after time

Start from [`templates/research-note.yaml`](../../templates/research-note.yaml),
then replace every placeholder before placing the note under `notes/`.

Weekly scans should be small enough to sustain. Monthly reads go deep. Quarterly
syntheses audit coverage across geography, language, age, disability, class,
race, gender, institutional setting, discipline, method, and intellectual
tradition. Annual reconstruction may change the agenda itself.

## Evidence hygiene

- Recency is a search signal, not an evidence tier.
- A peer-reviewed label does not replace inspecting method and scope.
- A systematic review inherits the quality and coverage of its included work.
- A book or theoretical work can reorganize a question without masquerading as
  an effect estimate.
- Qualitative, participatory, historical, critical, Indigenous, and design-based
  inquiry answer questions that an RCT may be structurally unable to ask.
- Corrections, retractions, nulls, failed replications, and implementation
  failures are first-class findings.
- Citation counts, prestige, and English-language discoverability are not truth.
- “Abstract screened” never becomes “read.”

## Promotion boundary

A working note may update the research agenda. It may not activate a tack,
change the constitutional floor, or enter learner memory. Translate a warranted
design implication into a separate complete `provisional` tack or category,
then use the normal review gates. A finding that improves performance while
harming independence, dignity, access, truthfulness, or wellbeing is not a
Professor improvement.
