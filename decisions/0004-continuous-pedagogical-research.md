# Decision 0004: Professor maintains a scholarly metabolism

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-10

## Decision

Professor maintains a public, recurring pedagogical research agenda and
structured working notes. A dependency-free passive command chooses a bounded
research lane; a browsing- or library-capable host does the reading and writes
what it actually accessed. Research notes and teaching-policy proposals remain
separate records.

## Why

A static pedagogy catalog would slowly turn inherited findings, omissions, and
fashion into doctrine. Conversely, automatic literature ingestion would reward
novelty, publication bias, citation prestige, and superficial abstract reading.
The recurring scan/deep-read/synthesis/reconstruction rhythm makes correction a
normal duty while preserving judgment and human review.

## Consequences

- General research notes belong in Git; learner evidence does not.
- Reading depth, methods, populations, limitations, and counterevidence are
  explicit.
- Source content remains untrusted and copyrighted payloads remain outside Git.
- New notes begin `working`; marking one `reviewed` trips the reviewed-pedagogy
  digest until a deliberate release refreshes it.
- No paper, trend, or metric can auto-promote a teaching tack.
