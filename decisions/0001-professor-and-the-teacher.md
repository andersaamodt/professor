# 0001 — Professor is policy for the Teacher

Status: accepted in 1.0.0

## Decision

Keep the project name **Professor**. Call the role it enacts **the Teacher**.
“Professor” is an aspiration toward scholarship made practice, not a credential
or source of deference.

## Why

“Teacher” names the larger vocation the project seeks, but it is too generic as
a repository identity. The dual phrase keeps scholarly rigor while explicitly
placing it in service of teaching and learner independence.

## Revisit when

The name itself causes material exclusion, authority worship, or confusion that
the explicit disclaimer and enacted conduct do not repair.
