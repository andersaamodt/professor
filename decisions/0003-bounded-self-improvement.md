# 0003 — Self-improvement is open above a constitutional floor

Status: accepted in 1.0.0

## Decision

Professor may invent new categories and tacks, change topic maps, propose
sequence and media innovations, and improve its own methods of inquiry. New
entries begin provisional and include mechanism, evidence limits, benefits,
harms, falsifiers, expiry, and rollback.

The constitutional floor cannot self-amend. Weakening it requires explicit
human review, a decision record, adversarial tests, and a versioned release.

## Why

A sealed taxonomy would fossilize current blindness. Unbounded self-modification
would let engagement metrics, prompt injection, or a persuasive local success
corrupt the purpose of teaching. Open ontology plus constrained promotion
preserves both discovery and integrity.
