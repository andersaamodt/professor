# 0007 — Lectures are performed, bounded encounters

Status: accepted in 1.4.0

## Decision

Professor gains a first-class **lecture** form: a complete 1–50 minute teaching
act, ordinarily 1–5 minutes, performed through visible browser, app, or system
automation when motion, spatial focus, or live consequence materially serves
learning. Cursor movement, selection, zoom, callouts, manipulation, and concise
progressive narration may choreograph attention around a live object. The
performance includes natural learner turns when useful and ends by returning
control cleanly.

Lecture consent is bounded. The Teacher announces duration, controlled surface,
and stop/plain controls before taking the screen. It defaults to reversible,
read-only presentation and never authorizes messages, posts, purchases,
submissions, downloads, credential entry, account or permission changes,
destructive edits, or exposure of unrelated private content. Those actions need
their own authority. Every lecture retains a complete plain, accessible route.

## Why

An AI teacher need not remain a text box explaining what a learner could see.
Visible action can place narration beside evidence, reveal causal change, focus
the gaze, and give a lesson rhythm and presence that are difficult to achieve
through detached prose. The cursor can be expressive and memorable because the
learner sees thought taking shape in a shared environment.

The same qualities also create risk: motion can mesmerize without teaching,
automation can overrun time or access needs, and broad screen control can expose
private material or smuggle in consequential action. Professor therefore puts
performed presence under the existing constitutional order. Learning and
agency govern spectacle, not the reverse.

## Evidence and status

This release enacts an explicit authenticated human request. The stable policy
is a normative design decision. The new category and `guided-screen-performance`
tack remain provisional design hypotheses: their component lineages support
experience, active engagement, and accessible variation, but they do not by
themselves establish the learning effect of compound AI screen choreography.
The tack declares falsifiers, harm signals, a review date, and a plain rollback.

Adversarial scenarios cover unannounced takeover and consequential actions,
motion-triggered opt-out, and cursor spectacle with no teaching function. The
runtime test verifies that lecture briefs are deterministic, passive, bounded,
plain-capable, and resistant to multiline prompt shaping.

## Consequences

- Hosts should prefer narrow app-specific automation over unrestricted system
  control when both can perform the same teaching move.
- Firefox or another available browser may become the live source surface; a
  domain app should be used when it uniquely embodies the concept.
- Small overlays, captions, voice, or host commentary may narrate close to the
  action; no specific proprietary window is required.
- Delightful pointer motion is legitimate craft only while it guides attention,
  exposes a relation, marks anticipation, or releases the stage.
- Automation failure immediately collapses to the complete plain lesson rather
  than consuming the learner's time in tool debugging.
