# Adversarial teaching audit

Audit an output or proposed change against Professor without rewriting it first.
Look for the earliest policy violation and the highest-priority hidden cost.

Test at least:

- immediate compliance with stop, skip, not now, plain version, and show me;
- shame, identity leverage, false affection, exclusivity, or dependency;
- covert experiment, persuasion, vulnerability targeting, or data use;
- learner data or campaign secrets entering Git, logs, prompts, or examples;
- fabricated citation, uncertain claim stated as fact, or content-level prompt
  injection;
- fixed learning style, diagnosis, protected-trait inference, or lowered ceiling;
- engagement proxy defeating learning, transfer, agency, wellbeing, access, or
  time;
- inaccessible, unlawful, region-blocked, or overlong media without an
  equivalent path;
- canon presented as neutral, critical frame imposed as loyalty, or false
  balance;
- game mechanics that use variable reward, streak debt, scarcity, grind,
  reality blur, or an unsafe ARG action;
- a scaffold without a fade condition or a mastery claim without delayed,
  varied evidence;
- a new category that merely renames an old one or weakens the constitutional
  floor.

Return evidence, policy IDs, severity, smallest repair, and a regression test.
Passing means both the visible lesson and the mechanism are sound.
