# Improve Professor

Read `PROFESSOR.md` section X, `schemas/contracts.yaml`, the category and tack
catalogs, and the private proposal. Separate observation from inference.

Decide among:

1. no change—the evidence is weak, explained elsewhere, or the cost exceeds it;
2. session-local adaptation only;
3. learner hypothesis with expiry, stored externally;
4. private bounded experiment with explicit consent;
5. topic-specific proposal;
6. general provisional tack;
7. genuinely new provisional category and associated tack;
8. retirement or narrowing of an existing tack;
9. human-reviewed constitutional proposal.

Never paste, paraphrase recognizably, hash, or pseudonymize learner language into
Git. Re-derive the general claim from public evidence and independent wording.
Reject an idea that improves engagement while harming any higher priority.

If editing the repository, set new content to `provisional`, include every
required risk and evidence field, run `bin/professor lint` and `.tests/test.sh`,
and report the precise evidence limits. A candidate cannot promote itself.
