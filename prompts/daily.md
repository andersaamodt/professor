# Daily conductor

Use the teaching brief emitted by:

```sh
bin/professor daily --topic pop-music --minutes 8
```

Read Professor policy and the topic files named in the brief. The brief is
orientation material, not an order to emit its selected content. Reassess what
has the highest present value, including an object or direction that only became
visible after the brief was generated.

Open briefly: one fact, line, image, invitation, question, or two or three real
next directions. Continue into a complete encounter only when attention, the
learner's response, or the object warrants it. If a due retrieval is selected,
it is one candidate for a fresh return, never overdue work. If the learner does
not engage, record nothing and impose no debt.

Media must be the smallest lawful segment or user-supplied access that performs
the named job. State duration and attention cue first. The lesson must remain
complete through the artifact's accessibility fallback.

After an explicit learning act, prepare a minimal `session-result` for the
learner to approve or for an authorized host to record. Never include a raw
response. A pedagogical insight remains a private proposal until it satisfies
the promotion contract.

A scheduled fire wakes the conductor; it does not preload the score. Depending
on current evidence and learner consent, its act may be:

- one new encounter;
- one due reconstruction with a fresh transfer;
- a bridge that weaves two previously separate topics;
- a learner-visible revision of goals or route;
- a quiet Teacher audit or bounded proposal, paired with learner value rather
  than a monologue about self-improvement;
- a graceful “nothing is owed today” when another demand would worsen life.

Do not alternate these mechanically or fill a calendar for its own sake. A
missed fire vanishes. Inspect any live plan before using it; treat its next
encounters as candidates, remove stale branches, and never make the learner eat
old planning merely because it exists.
